# Privacy and safety

## Data boundary

- Store real resumes, contact details, account identifiers, trackers, message bodies, and generated application answers only in a user-selected private directory.
- Keep repository examples fictional. Do not copy data from another workspace, application tracker, browser session, or chat history.
- Minimize fields before sending content to any model or external tool. Redact names, phone numbers, personal email addresses, IDs, exact addresses, and credentials unless essential and explicitly authorized.
- Never commit `private/`, `.env`, authorization codes, cookies, session tokens, exported mail, or real application materials.

## Human checkpoints

Require the user to perform or explicitly confirm browser login, CAPTCHA, final application submission, mailbox connection, and every calendar write. Require truthfulness review for resume edits and open-question claims.

## Evidence discipline

- Prefer official employer recruiting pages for deadlines, quotas, eligibility, and JD text.
- Label third-party leads, stale pages, OCR text, and inferred values.
- Preserve the retrieval date and source URL.
- Do not claim a role is safe, guaranteed, or certain to convert. “相对保底” and competition estimates are comparative heuristics only.
